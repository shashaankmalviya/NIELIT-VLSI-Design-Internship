# Analog Design

This directory contains the analog circuit design assignments and mini-project completed during the **Internship on VLSI Design Covering Analog and Digital Design Flow**, organized by NIT and NIELIT Calicut.

The work focuses on CMOS circuit design, transistor characterization, amplifier design, operational amplifiers, and analog circuit analysis using **Cadence Virtuoso**.

## Contents

| **Folder** | **Topics Covered** |
|---|---|
| [01_MOSFET_Characterization](./01_MOSFET_Characterization) | DC characterization of NMOS and PMOS transistors, drain current analysis, operating regions, and transistor characteristics |
| [02_CMOS_Inverter](./02_CMOS_Inverter) | CMOS inverter design, voltage transfer characteristics, switching behaviour, transient response, and power analysis |
| [03_CS_Amplifier](./03_CS_Amplifier) | Common Source amplifier design, voltage gain, frequency response, and transistor operating point analysis |
| [04_Single_Stage_CMOS_OpAmp](./04_Single_Stage_CMOS_OpAmp) | Single-stage CMOS operational amplifier design, transistor sizing, DC operating point, open-loop gain, and frequency response |
| [05_Two_Stage_CMOS_OpAmp](./05_Two_Stage_CMOS_OpAmp) | Two-stage CMOS operational amplifier design, gain enhancement, frequency compensation, stability, and transient analysis |
| [Mini_Project_Two_Stage_CMOS_OpAmp](./Mini_Project_Two_Stage_CMOS_OpAmp) | Two-stage CMOS operational amplifier mini-project covering transistor sizing, biasing, frequency compensation, AC analysis, phase margin, slew rate, and power consumption |

## Tools and Technologies

- **Cadence Virtuoso** – Schematic design and circuit simulation
- **LTspice** – Analog circuit simulation and analysis
- **CMOS Technology** – NMOS and PMOS transistor-based circuit design

## Overview

These assignments and the mini-project provided practical experience in analog VLSI design, progressing from individual MOSFET characterization to CMOS amplifier and operational amplifier design, simulation, and performance analysis.
